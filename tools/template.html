Game Compatibility List

Every game the nxnative engine has a profile for, plus games people report in its issues, with how well it runs, where it runs, the update it wants, and, once you choose your games folder, which ones you have. Sources: the engine's dm/games profiles, NX2Apple's GAMES.md, and every open and closed issue in nxnative and NX2Apple.

A finding marked fixed comes from an issue closed as completed. Hover a title ID to see where its profile comes from. Recommended game update is the game's own update (from Nintendo) that the nxnative profile is built for; Profile updated is the date of the last commit to that profile in nxnative; for a game with no profile it is the date its newest issue was opened. The sources are checked automatically once a day, and the list is updated when something changed.

    Your games

    See which games on the list you have, and whether your game updates match. Choose the folder that holds your Switch games: .nsp, .nsz, .xci, .xcz and .nup files, and Switch 2 dump folders. This page reads only their names, in this browser.

    Reads

    The names of the files and folders inside the folder you choose, such as Super Mario Odyssey [0100000000010000][v393216].nsp. That is how it finds title IDs and update versions.

    Never reads or sends

    The contents of any file: no game data, keys or saves. Nothing about your games leaves your browser; there is no upload, account or analytics. The page only downloads its fonts from Google Fonts.

    Keeps

    The matched games, their versions and the names it could not match, in this browser's local storage, so they are here next time. Forget my games deletes them.

    Your browser may ask to “upload” the folder, or to let the site “view files”. That is its standard wording for letting a page see a folder's file names; nothing is sent anywhere.
